### 4.5 Medical Records & Prescriptions

**Description:** Give doctors fast access to the history of the patients they are treating, let them record consultations and prescriptions, and give patients read-only access to their own records, while keeping every record permanent and versioned.

| Req ID | Requirement (The system shall…) | Type | Priority | Source/Stakeholder | Acceptance criteria / Test case ref | Comments / Dependencies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HAP-F-040 | The system shall show a Doctor their own appointments for a chosen day in time order and shall let the Doctor open the chart (profile, allergies, past visits, consultation notes, prescriptions) only of patients who have or had an appointment with that doctor. | Functional | High | Doctors, Patients | AC-HAP-F-040: The day list shows only that doctor's appointments, sorted by time; opening the chart of a listed patient returns 200 within 2 s with allergies at the top; requesting the chart of a patient with no appointment with that doctor returns 403 `NO_CARE_RELATIONSHIP` and an audit entry. Test: TC-MR-03 | Care-relationship rule implements HAP-SR-004. Every chart view is logged (HAP-SR-007). |
| HAP-F-041 | The system shall let a Doctor record a consultation (symptoms, vitals: blood pressure, pulse, temperature, weight; diagnosis; notes) for an appointment that is CHECKED_IN, and saving it shall set the appointment to COMPLETED. | Functional | High | Doctors | AC-HAP-F-041: Saving a consultation for a CHECKED_IN appointment returns 201 and the appointment becomes COMPLETED; saving for a BOOKED appointment returns 409 `APPOINTMENT_NOT_CHECKED_IN`; vitals outside physical limits (e.g. pulse > 300) return 422. Test: TC-MR-01 | Diagnosis and notes are encrypted at rest (HAP-SR-006). |
| HAP-F-042 | The system shall let a Doctor issue a prescription linked to the appointment, with one or more items each having medicine name, dosage, frequency, duration in days and instructions, and shall generate a prescription PDF showing clinic, doctor, patient MRN, date and items. | Functional | High | Doctors, Patients, Pharmacists | AC-HAP-F-042: A prescription with 3 items returns 201 and a PDF of 3 items is downloadable; an item without dosage or duration returns 422; the PDF is generated in ≤ 3 s. Test: TC-MR-02 | ReportLab is used for PDFs. |
| HAP-F-043 | The system shall let a Patient view their own visits, consultation summaries and prescriptions read-only and download any of their prescription PDFs. | Functional | High | Patients | AC-HAP-F-043: The patient sees all their own prescriptions in date order; any PUT, POST or DELETE by a Patient on records returns 403; a patient cannot open another patient's prescription (403). Test: TC-MR-02 | Patients do not see the doctor's private notes field. |
| HAP-F-044 | The system shall never delete medical records; editing a consultation or prescription shall create a new version with the editing doctor and timestamp, and all earlier versions shall remain viewable. | Functional | High | Doctors, Hospital management, Regulators | AC-HAP-F-044: Editing a consultation once produces version 2; `GET` returns version 2 by default and lists version 1 with its author and time; there is no DELETE endpoint for records. Test: TC-MR-01 | Supports HAP-SO-2 (integrity and accountability). |
