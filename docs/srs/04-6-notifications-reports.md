### 4.6 Notifications & Reports

**Description:** Keep patients informed about every change to their appointments and give the Admin a live view of clinic activity plus exportable reports.

| Req ID | Requirement (The system shall…) | Type | Priority | Source/Stakeholder | Acceptance criteria / Test case ref | Comments / Dependencies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HAP-F-050 | The system shall notify the patient by email and in-app message within 1 minute of an appointment being booked, rescheduled or cancelled, or of the doctor marking leave on that date, and shall send a reminder 24 hours (± 5 minutes) before the appointment start. | Functional | High | Patients, Reception | AC-HAP-F-050: For each of the four events an email (captured in MailHog during testing) and an in-app notification exist within 60 s; the reminder is sent between 23 h 55 min and 24 h 05 min before the slot start; no notification contains the diagnosis or prescription text. Test: TC-NOT-01 | Uses the Email Gateway (SMTP) and APScheduler. Failed emails are retried 3 times at 5-minute intervals. |
| HAP-F-051 | The system shall show the Admin a dashboard with today's appointments per doctor (by status), the no-show rate for the last 30 days and each doctor's slot utilisation percentage for the current week. | Functional | Medium | Admin, Hospital management | AC-HAP-F-051: Dashboard figures equal the values computed directly from the database for a seeded data set; the dashboard loads in ≤ 2 s. Test: covered in sprint testing | Utilisation = booked or completed slots ÷ generated slots × 100. |
| HAP-F-052 | The system shall let the Admin export an appointment report for a chosen date range (maximum 366 days) as a CSV file with one row per appointment. | Functional | Low | Admin | AC-HAP-F-052: The CSV opens in a spreadsheet and has the columns date, time, doctor, department, MRN, status; the row count equals the number of appointments in the range; no clinical notes are included; the export is written to the audit log. Test: covered in sprint testing | Depends on HAP-SR-007 (export is an admin action). |
