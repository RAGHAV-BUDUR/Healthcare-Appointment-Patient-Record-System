### 4.4 Appointment Management

**Description:** Let patients and receptionists find a free slot and book it, and guarantee that no doctor is ever double-booked and no patient holds two overlapping appointments, even when several people try to book the same slot at the same moment.

| Req ID | Requirement (The system shall…) | Type | Priority | Source/Stakeholder | Acceptance criteria / Test case ref | Comments / Dependencies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HAP-F-030 | The system shall let a Patient search active doctors by specialization or name and show the doctor's FREE slots for a chosen date up to 30 days ahead. | Functional | High | Patients | AC-HAP-F-030: Searching "Cardiology" lists only active cardiologists; choosing a doctor and date lists exactly the FREE slots for that day in time order; BOOKED, BLOCKED and past slots are not shown. Test: TC-APT-01 | Slots come from the Doctor & Schedule Service (HAP-F-021). Performance target HAP-NF-002. |
| HAP-F-031 | The system shall let a Patient book one FREE slot with a reason for visit (5–300 characters), creating an appointment with status BOOKED that appears in the doctor's schedule. | Functional | High | Patients, Doctors | AC-HAP-F-031: Booking returns 201 with the appointment ID and status BOOKED; the slot status changes to BOOKED; the appointment appears in the doctor's daily list within 5 s; a confirmation is sent (HAP-F-050). Test: TC-APT-01 | Reason text is visible to the doctor only. |
| HAP-F-032 | The system shall prevent appointment conflicts: a slot shall hold at most one active (BOOKED or CHECKED_IN) appointment, a patient shall not hold two active appointments that overlap in time, and when several bookings for the same slot arrive at the same time exactly one shall succeed and the others shall receive 409 `SLOT_TAKEN`. | Functional | High | Doctors, Patients, Hospital management | AC-HAP-F-032: 20 concurrent booking requests for one slot produce exactly one 201 and nineteen 409 `SLOT_TAKEN`, and the database holds one active appointment for that slot; booking a slot that overlaps the patient's existing active appointment with another doctor returns 409 `PATIENT_OVERLAP`. Test: TC-APT-02 | Implemented with a row lock on the slot inside one transaction plus a partial unique index (SAD §3.6). |
| HAP-F-033 | The system shall let a Patient reschedule an appointment to another FREE slot, or cancel it, up to 2 hours before the slot start; the old slot shall become FREE immediately; later changes shall be rejected with 409 `CHANGE_WINDOW_CLOSED`. | Functional | High | Patients, Reception | AC-HAP-F-033: Cancelling 3 hours before start returns 200, status CANCELLED and the slot is FREE in the next search; cancelling 1 hour 59 minutes before start returns 409 `CHANGE_WINDOW_CLOSED`; rescheduling applies the same conflict checks as HAP-F-032. Test: TC-APT-03 | Receptionist changes inside the 2-hour window are allowed (HAP-F-035). |
| HAP-F-034 | The system shall track each appointment through the statuses BOOKED → CHECKED_IN → COMPLETED, or BOOKED → CANCELLED / NO_SHOW; the Receptionist shall check patients in; the system shall mark an appointment NO_SHOW 30 minutes after the slot end if it was never checked in; any other transition shall return 409 `INVALID_STATUS_TRANSITION`. | Functional | High | Reception, Doctors, Admin | AC-HAP-F-034: Check-in changes BOOKED to CHECKED_IN; an appointment not checked in is NO_SHOW within 31 minutes after the slot end; COMPLETED → BOOKED and CANCELLED → CHECKED_IN both return 409. Test: TC-APT-03 | COMPLETED is set by the Medical Record Service when notes are saved (HAP-F-041). |
| HAP-F-035 | The system shall let a Receptionist book, reschedule and cancel appointments on behalf of any registered patient, applying the same conflict rules as HAP-F-032, and shall record which staff member made the change. | Functional | Medium | Reception | AC-HAP-F-035: A Receptionist booking for a patient creates a BOOKED appointment with `created_by` = the receptionist's user ID; a conflicting booking returns 409 as for patients. Test: covered in sprint testing | Supports phone and walk-in bookings. |
