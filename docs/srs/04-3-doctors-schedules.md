### 4.3 Doctor & Schedule Management

**Description:** Maintain doctor profiles and turn each doctor's weekly working hours into bookable time slots, so that patients can only book times when the doctor is actually available.

| Req ID | Requirement (The system shall…) | Type | Priority | Source/Stakeholder | Acceptance criteria / Test case ref | Comments / Dependencies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HAP-F-020 | The system shall let an Admin add and edit doctor profiles with name, specialization, department, qualification and slot duration (10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes; default 15). | Functional | High | Admin, Hospital management | AC-HAP-F-020: A new doctor profile is linked to a Doctor account and appears in patient search within 1 minute; a slot duration outside the four allowed values returns 422; changes to the slot duration apply only to slots generated afterwards. Test: covered in sprint testing | Doctor account is created first (HAP-F-003). Edits are audit-logged. |
| HAP-F-021 | The system shall let a Doctor define weekly availability as one or more time ranges per weekday and shall generate FREE slots of the doctor's slot duration for the next 30 days, regenerating them every night. | Functional | High | Doctors, Patients | AC-HAP-F-021: Availability Monday 09:00–12:00 with 15-minute slots produces exactly 12 slots on every Monday in the next 30 days, starting 09:00, 09:15 … 11:45; overlapping time ranges for the same day are rejected with 422; slots that already have appointments are never removed by regeneration. Test: TC-DOC-01 | Slots are the booking inventory used by the Appointment Service (HAP-F-030–032). |
| HAP-F-022 | The system shall let a Doctor mark full or partial leave dates; all FREE slots in that period shall become BLOCKED, and every patient already BOOKED in that period shall be notified and asked to reschedule. | Functional | High | Doctors, Patients, Reception | AC-HAP-F-022: After marking leave for a date, slot search for that doctor and date returns no FREE slots; each affected BOOKED patient receives a notification within 1 minute (HAP-F-050); affected appointments are listed for the Receptionist. Test: TC-DOC-01 | Existing appointments are not cancelled automatically, so the patient can choose a new time. |
