### 4.2 Patient Management

**Description:** Create and maintain one accurate record per patient, identified by a unique Patient ID (MRN), whether the patient registers online or is registered by the Receptionist at the front desk.

| Req ID | Requirement (The system shall…) | Type | Priority | Source/Stakeholder | Acceptance criteria / Test case ref | Comments / Dependencies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HAP-F-010 | The system shall store for each patient: full name, date of birth, gender, mobile number, email, address, blood group, known allergies and an emergency contact (name and phone), and shall generate a unique, never-reused Patient ID (MRN) in the format `HAP-P-` followed by 6 digits. | Functional | High | Patients, Reception, Doctors | AC-HAP-F-010: Saving a profile with all mandatory fields (name, date of birth, gender, mobile) returns 201 with a new MRN; a missing mandatory field returns 422 `VALIDATION_ERROR` naming the field; a date of birth in the future is rejected; 1,000 registrations produce 1,000 distinct MRNs. Test: TC-PAT-01 | Allergies are shown on the doctor's chart view (HAP-F-040). Mobile number: 10 digits. |
| HAP-F-011 | The system shall let a Patient view and edit their own contact details, address, allergies and emergency contact, but not their MRN or date of birth, and shall let a Receptionist register a walk-in patient and edit any patient's contact details. | Functional | High | Patients, Reception | AC-HAP-F-011: A patient's edit of their phone number is saved and visible on next login; an attempt to change MRN or date of birth via the API returns 403; a Receptionist-created patient can later be linked to a login by email verification. Test: TC-PAT-01 | Date of birth corrections are done by the Admin and are audit-logged (HAP-SR-007). |
| HAP-F-012 | The system shall warn the user before creating a patient whose mobile number and date of birth both match an existing patient, and shall show the matching MRN. | Functional | Medium | Reception, Medical records | AC-HAP-F-012: Registering a second patient with the same mobile number and date of birth shows a warning with the existing MRN; the record is created only if the user confirms "create anyway". Test: covered in sprint testing | Prevents split medical histories for one person. |
| HAP-F-013 | The system shall let a Receptionist or Admin search patients by name (partial match, case-insensitive), MRN or mobile number, returning at most 50 results per page in ≤ 1 s. | Functional | Medium | Reception, Admin | AC-HAP-F-013: With 10,000 patients in the database, a search returns the correct matches in ≤ 1 s (95th percentile); results are paged at 50; a Patient or Doctor token receives 403. Test: covered in sprint testing | Doctors find patients through their appointment list instead (HAP-F-040). |
